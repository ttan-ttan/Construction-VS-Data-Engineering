[ 02. STAGE-BY-STAGE EQUIVALENCE ]

PHASE 01

Business Brief & Requirement Analysis

Civil Construction Building Specs

Defining Physical Constraints

Before breaking ground, engineers evaluate client intent: occupancy type, structural load parameters (dead/live load), 4-hour fire resistance ratings, and building authority codes (e.g., BCA/SCDF).

Data Engineering Business Discovery

Defining Analytical Scope

Engineers clarify business objectives: key performance indicators (KPIs), latency requirements (real-time vs batch), query performance target, and data security governance (GDPR/PDPA).

PHASE 02

Extraction & Ingestion

Civil Construction Document Ingestion

Gathering Specifications & Drawings

Consolidating architectural blueprints, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) drawings, soil test reports, and standard structural details across multiple contractor channels.

Data Engineering Pipeline Ingestion (E)

Sourcing Raw Data Streams

Pulling unstructured and structured data from disparate source systems: transactional SQL databases, RESTful APIs, cloud storage buckets, web hooks, and legacy flat files into a raw Landing Zone.

PHASE 03

Transformation & Clash Detection

Civil Construction BIM Coordination

Resolving Spatial & Regulatory Clashes

Overlapping MEP ducts with structural beams (clash resolution). Adjusting beam dimensions, ensuring fire resistance compliance, and standardizing details prior to site execution.

Data Engineering Transformation (T)

Data Cleansing & Modeling

Cleansing dirty data, handling missing values, deduplicating records, modeling star schemas (fact & dimension tables), and computing aggregated DAX metrics to ensure analytical accuracy.

PHASE 04

Sign-off & Production Delivery

Civil Construction Site Execution

PFT & Physical Erection

Obtaining Qualified Person (QP) / Consultant approval, issuing final Construction (PFT) drawings to the site team, pouring concrete, and delivering the physical facility to the client.

Data Engineering Target Load (L) & BI

Warehouse Serving & Dashboards

Loading curated Gold-layer schemas into cloud warehouses (Snowflake/Fabric/Postgres), triggering automated Power BI/Tableau reports, and enabling executive decision-making.
